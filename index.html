Questions

Common questions

What do I need to send?

One month's card-processing statement and one waste-hauling bill, by email to hello@feeright.com or as a reply to any of my emails.

What does the findings sheet cost?

Nothing. You get it within three business days, on one page, with every number tied to a line on your bills.

Do I have to switch my processor or hauler?

No. If there's a saving, I ask your current vendors to lower the price, after you approve it in writing.

How is the fee worked out?

You pay half of each month's saving for 36 months, once it shows on your bill. Refunds of past overcharges are split 50/50. Nothing otherwise.

How do warranty rates work?

I prepare the filing, and you pay half of the increase the manufacturer approves, for its first 12 months, and nothing if it isn't approved.

Will you need my card numbers, passwords or logins?

Never. Bills are kept in one place and deleted on request.

I'm a dealer's accountant. Can my client use this?

Yes. They can send one month's card statement and hauling bill to hello@feeright.com, and the findings sheet is free for them too.
