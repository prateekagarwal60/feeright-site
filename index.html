Card-processing and waste bills, checked for car dealers.

Feeright checks car dealers' card-processing and waste-hauling bills, and their warranty rates.

How it works

Send one month's card statement and hauling bill. Within three business days you get a free one-page findings sheet, every number tied to a line on your bills.

If there's a saving, I ask your current vendors to lower the price, after you approve it in writing.

What it costs

  • You pay half of each month's saving for 36 months, once it shows on your bill.
  • Refunds of past overcharges are split 50/50.
  • Nothing otherwise.

Warranty rates

I prepare the filing, and you pay half of the increase the manufacturer approves, for its first 12 months, and nothing if it isn't approved.

Who you work with: an engineer (IIT Delhi CS), ex-Amazon and Microsoft. You deal with me directly.

I never ask for card numbers, passwords or logins; bills are kept in one place and deleted on request.
